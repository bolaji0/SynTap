Why it matters

Make asking for reviews easier.

A happy customer means to leave a review, then gets busy and forgets. Searching for your business name, finding the right listing, and scrolling to "Write a review" is enough friction to lose it. A Syntap Review stand removes that search entirely.

Ask at the exact moment they're happiest — right after the meal, the haircut, or the service.

How it works

Three steps. No app. No searching.

01

Customer taps

The customer holds their phone near the stand for a second — the same motion as a contactless card payment.

02

Review page opens

Their phone opens straight to your Google review page. No searching for your business name.

03

Customer leaves a review

They write a few words and post it — while the experience is still fresh.

No NFC on their phone? No problem. Every stand also carries a QR code that opens the same review page when scanned.
